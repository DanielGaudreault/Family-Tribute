Anita Pépin

1939 – July 1, 2018
Age 78
Angliers, Québec

The Robert et Fils Funeral Home of Témiscamingue announced the passing of Mrs. Anita Pépin of Angliers, beloved wife of the late Raymond Gaudreault. She passed away on July 1, 2018, at the age of 78.

The family welcomed relatives and friends on September 1 at Saint-Viateur Church in Angliers to celebrate her life and pay their final respects. Following the funeral service, Anita was laid to rest in the cemetery of the same parish.

A loving wife, mother, grandmother, sister and friend whose memory continues to live on through the many lives she touched.
