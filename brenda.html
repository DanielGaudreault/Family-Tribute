Brenda Louise Rosseau

July 4, 1961 - September 15, 2024
Age 63
Windsor, Ontario

We are sad to announce that, on September 15, 2024, at the age of 63, Brenda Louise Rosseau of Windsor, Ontario passed away.

As we bid Brenda farewell, let us not mourn the absence of her physical form, but rather celebrate the essence of her spirit that lingers in the spaces between moments.

For Brenda is not truly gone, but merely transformed, her energy intertwined with the fabric of the universe itself, forever dancing among the stars.
