Brenda Louise Rosseau

July 4, 1961 - September 15, 2024
Windsor, Ontario

We are saddened to announce the passing of Brenda Louise Rosseau on September 15, 2024, at the age of 63.

Brenda's love, kindness and strength continue to live through family and friends whose lives she touched.

"For Brenda is not truly gone, but merely transformed, her energy intertwined with the fabric of the universe, forever dancing among the stars."
