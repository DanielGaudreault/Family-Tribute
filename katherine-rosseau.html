Katherine Rosseau

1963 – August 9, 2006
Age 43

Katherine Rosseau passed away on Wednesday, August 9, 2006, at the age of 43. She was the cherished daughter of the late Wallace Rosseau and the late Lois Brown Rosseau.

Katherine will always be remembered for her love of family, her strength, her caring nature, and the many lives she touched throughout her life. Her memory continues to live on through her children, grandchildren, family members, and friends.

"Forever loved, forever remembered, and forever a part of our family story."
